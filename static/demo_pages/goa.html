Destination Overview

Goa is India's premier beach and coastal holiday destination, renowned for its golden coastline stretching along the Arabian Sea, vibrant Portuguese-colonial heritage architecture, lush spice plantations, and fresh seafood. Divided into North Goa (famed for bustling beaches, nightlife, flea markets, and water sports) and South Goa (celebrated for tranquil sands, luxury resorts, and historic churches), it is a versatile getaway for couples, families, and solo explorers.

Best Time to Visit: November to March (Pleasant, sunny, 22°C - 31°C).
Local Transport: Rented scooters (₹350 - ₹500/day), self-drive hatchbacks (₹1,200 - ₹1,800/day), GoaMiles app cabs, and local buses.

Old GoaUNESCO Heritage
Entry: Free (Donations voluntary)
Timings: 09:00 AM - 06:30 PM (Sundays open 10:30 AM)

Candolim / SinquerimSea View
Entry: ₹50 per person

17th-century Portuguese fort perched atop a cliff overlooking Sinquerim Beach and the mouth of the Mandovi River. Offers panoramic sunset views and a four-storey lighthouse.

Practical Travel Tips & Transport

  • Scooter & Car Rentals: Always carry a valid driver's license. Helmets are mandatory on state and national highways. Expect to pay ₹350 - ₹500/day for Honda Activa scooters.
  • Cash vs Digital: UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe) is accepted in 95% of cafes, shacks, and shops. Carry ₹1,500 - ₹2,000 in cash for parking and beach gear.
  • Local Etiquette: Wear modest clothing when visiting religious sites (churches and temples). Swimwear is restricted to beach shores.
