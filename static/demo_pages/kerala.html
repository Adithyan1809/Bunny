Destination Overview

Kerala is a tropical paradise situated on the southwestern Malabar Coast of India. Known for palm-fringed backwaters of Alleppey (Alappuzha), sprawling tea gardens of Munnar, historic spice trading ports in Fort Kochi, and cliffside beaches in Varkala.

Best Time to Visit: September to March.
Local Transport: State water transport ferries (₹15 - ₹40), KSRTC buses, prepaid taxis, and auto-rickshaws.

Verified Popular Attractions & Things to Do

Alleppey Backwaters Canoe / Shikhara Cruise

AlappuzhaBackwaters
Shikhara Boat: ₹600 - ₹800/hour for 2-4 persons
Timings: 06:00 AM - 06:00 PM

Cruise through narrow, peaceful canals lined with paddy fields, water lilies, coir-making villages, and rustic waterside toddy shops.

Eravikulam National Park (Munnar)

MunnarWildlife & Trekking
Entry: ₹200 (Indians) / ₹500 (Foreigners)
Timings: 07:30 AM - 04:00 PM (Closed Feb-March for calving)

Home of the endangered Nilgiri Tahr mountain goat and rolling shola grasslands beneath Anamudi, South India's highest peak.

Fort Kochi & Chinese Fishing Nets

KochiHeritage Walk
Entry: Free walking area
Timings: Open 24 Hours | Net operation sunrise to sunset

Stroll past 500-year-old Portuguese houses, Jew Town spice warehouses, Paradesi Synagogue, and beachside cantilevered Chinese fishing nets.
