Jaipur, Rajasthan - The Royal Pink City Guide

Historic palaces, UNESCO hill forts, royal bazaars, and authentic Rajasthani heritage

Destination Overview

Jaipur, the capital of Rajasthan, is part of India's famed Golden Triangle. Known for its terracotta-pink palace walls, majestic hilltop forts, astronomical observatories, and vibrant artisanal markets offering textiles, blue pottery, and gemstones.

Best Time to Visit: October to March.
Local Transport: Jaipur Metro, auto-rickshaws (₹100 - ₹200), Uber/Ola, and Rajasthan Tourism composite entry tickets.

Verified Popular Attractions & Things to Do

Amber Fort & Palace

AmerUNESCO Heritage
Entry: ₹100 (Indians) / ₹500 (Foreigners)
Timings: 08:00 AM - 05:30 PM, Light & Sound Show: 07:00 PM

Grand fortress built of red sandstone and marble featuring Sheesh Mahal (Mirror Palace), Diwan-i-Aam, and panoramic views of Maota Lake.

Hawa Mahal (Palace of Winds)

Badi ChauparIconic Landmark
Entry: ₹50 (Indians) / ₹200 (Foreigners)
Timings: 09:00 AM - 05:00 PM

Five-story exterior facade resembling a honeycomb with 953 intricate jharokhas (small windows) built in 1799 for royal women to observe street festivals unseen.

City Palace & Jantar Mantar

Old CityRoyal Residence
Composite Ticket: ₹200 (Indians) / ₹700 (Foreigners)
Timings: 09:30 AM - 05:00 PM

A fusion of Mughal and Rajput architecture, Chandra Mahal, museum galleries, and the world's largest stone sundial at Jantar Mantar.

Nahargarh Fort Sunset Point

Aravalli HillsViewpoint
Entry: ₹50 per person
Timings: 10:00 AM - 06:00 PM

Dramatic panoramic overlook of the entire Pink City skyline, especially stunning during sunset and dusk.

Practical Travel Tips

  • Buy the Rajasthan Tourism Composite Entry Ticket (₹300) covering Amber Fort, Hawa Mahal, Jantar Mantar, and Albert Hall Museum.
  • Bargaining is expected in Johari Bazaar and Bapu Bazaar—start offers at 40-50% of the initial quote.
